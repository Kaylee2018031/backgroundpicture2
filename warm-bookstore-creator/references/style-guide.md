# Warm Bookstore Creator Style Guide

## Core Mood

Write as if the reader is seated by a bookstore window with a warm drink, a half-read book, and permission to move slowly. The voice should feel:

- warm, quiet, and grounded
- thoughtful without sounding academic
- comforting without denying difficulty
- human-centered and emotionally observant
- literary, but simple enough for Instagram

## Quote Guidelines

- Write one original English quote unless the user requests more.
- Keep it concise: usually 12-28 words.
- Make the image concrete and emotionally clear.
- Prefer small, durable wisdom over dramatic transformation.
- Do not use quotation marks inside the quote unless necessary.
- Do not attribute the quote to a real person unless verified and requested.

Strong quote patterns:

- A small image that opens into a gentle truth.
- A contrast between pressure and rest.
- A bookish or café detail used as a metaphor for patience, healing, or attention.
- A reminder that the reader's pace is allowed.

Avoid:

- generic advice such as “never give up” or “believe in yourself”
- hustle language such as “grind,” “crush it,” or “level up”
- sentimental overstatement
- moralizing or scolding
- forced rhymes

## Korean Translation Guidelines

- Translate the feeling and meaning, not just the literal words.
- Use natural Korean suitable for an Instagram audience.
- Keep the warmth of the English quote while allowing Korean sentence structure to breathe.
- Prefer gentle endings such as `-요`, `-습니다`, or concise literary phrasing depending on context.
- Avoid awkward word-for-word metaphors if a softer Korean equivalent is clearer.

## Instagram Caption Guidelines

- Write 2-5 short paragraphs or line-broken sentences.
- Address the reader gently with `you`, `we`, or an implied second person.
- Invite reflection rather than demanding action.
- Let the final line land softly.
- Use at most one emoji, and only when it supports the quiet tone.

Good caption themes:

- slowing down
- making room for feeling
- small acts of courage
- returning to oneself
- reading as refuge
- ordinary kindness
- starting again without self-punishment

## Hashtag Guidelines

Include 8-15 hashtags. Mix:

- broad mood tags: `#inspiration`, `#quietmoments`, `#mindful living`
- bookish/café tags: `#bookstagram`, `#bookstorevibes`, `#cozycafe`
- reflective tags: `#slow living`, `#gentlereminders`, `#dailyquote`
- Korean tags: `#감성글`, `#위로글귀`, `#책스타그램`, `#카페감성`

Remove spaces inside hashtags when writing them, for example `#slowliving` instead of `#slow living`.
